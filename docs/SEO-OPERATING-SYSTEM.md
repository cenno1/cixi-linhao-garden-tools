# LINHAO Garden SEO / GEO Daily Operating System

Last updated: 2026-08-20

## Objective

Grow `https://linhaogarden.com` into a trustworthy B2B garden-tools and brass-hose-fitting resource that earns qualified discovery and inquiries from Google Search and AI-assisted search.

Rankings and indexing are not guaranteed. Every action must be useful to buyers, technically valid, factually supportable, and measurable.

## Decision hierarchy

1. Qualified organic inquiries
2. High-quality indexing of commercial pages
3. Visibility for non-brand, buyer-intent queries
4. AI-citable, first-hand product and sourcing information
5. Raw traffic

## KPI scorecard

### Primary KPI 1 — Qualified organic inquiries

- Definition: inquiries from organic search that include a real company or contact method, a relevant product, and a purchasing requirement.
- Sources: CRM or inquiry log + GA4 `generate_lead`; WhatsApp conversations are validated manually.
- Drivers: organic landing-page sessions, `whatsapp_click`, form completion rate, response time.
- Guardrail: do not count spam, test submissions, job seekers, or irrelevant consumer requests.

### Primary KPI 2 — High-quality commercial-page index rate

- Formula: Google-indexed canonical commercial URLs / all submitted canonical commercial URLs.
- Sources: Search Console Page indexing, URL inspection samples, and `sitemap.xml`.
- A URL counts only if it returns 200, is canonical, indexable, unique enough to deserve indexing, and has a working inquiry path.
- Guardrail: never create thin pages merely to raise the URL count.

### Primary KPI 3 — Buyer-intent search visibility

- Definition: non-brand purchasing queries with impressions and a best average position in the top 20, reported separately for positions 1–3, 4–10, and 11–20.
- Sources: Search Console query and landing-page exports.
- Priority query families: brass hose connectors, brass quick connectors, brass hose fittings, hose splitters, watering nozzles, OEM garden tools, garden hand-tool kits, and supplier/manufacturer variants.
- Guardrail: impressions must be relevant to B2B purchasing intent; unrelated traffic does not count as progress.

## Provisional stage targets

Targets are recalibrated after 28 days of reliable Search Console and GA4 data.

### First 30 days

- Establish a clean baseline for all three primary KPIs.
- Resolve every P0 crawl, canonical, sitemap, structured-data, mobile, and analytics issue.
- Audit the 12 highest-value commercial pages for unique images, specifications, buyer questions, internal links, and inquiry paths.
- Reach at least 80% high-quality commercial-page index coverage as a directional target.
- Keep all inquiry and WhatsApp analytics events testable.

### Days 31–60

- Reach at least 90% high-quality commercial-page index coverage as a directional target.
- Substantially improve 8–12 high-intent pages; do not publish filler.
- Add at least two evidence-led comparison, selection, compatibility, or procurement resources.
- Run the weekly position 8–20 opportunity loop whenever Search Console has sufficient data.

### Days 61–90

- Reach at least 95% high-quality commercial-page index coverage as a directional target.
- Build two coherent topic clusters with crawlable internal links.
- Use 3–5 relevant top-20 queries as an early traction signal, not a promise.
- Improve qualified organic inquiries and organic landing-page conversion against the 28-day baseline.

## Daily loop

Complete one highest-value, verifiable primary action per day. Check previous work first so the system does not create duplicate pages, duplicate pull requests, or repeated indexing requests.

### Monday — Indexing and Search Console

- Check correct property: `linhaogarden.com`.
- Review indexing changes, sitemap health, crawl blockers, and priority URLs.
- Review queries/pages with meaningful impressions and positions 8–20.
- Choose one indexation or opportunity-page action.

### Tuesday — Product SEO

- Improve one high-value product or category page.
- Confirm title, H1, canonical, description, unique product image, meaningful alt text, verified model/specifications, buyer-use cases, FAQs, related products, and inquiry CTA.
- Map one primary buyer intent to one canonical page; prevent keyword cannibalization.

### Wednesday — Original buyer evidence

- Create or improve one non-commodity asset based on LINHAO's own knowledge: compatibility table, material comparison, installation guide, packaging options, QC checklist, sample workflow, or sourcing FAQ.
- List missing business facts for the owner instead of inventing them.

### Thursday — Technical SEO and AI readability

- Test rendered HTML, structured data, crawlable links, sitemap/robots, mobile layout, image delivery, performance, and visible contact paths.
- Make answers easy to extract with clear headings, concise direct answers, tables where useful, and explicit entity/product relationships.
- Do not create `llms.txt` or mass-produce query-variant pages merely as an AI-search tactic.

### Friday — Conversion and lead quality

- Review GA4 `generate_lead` and `whatsapp_click`, inquiry flow, form errors, CTA clarity, mobile tap targets, and trust signals.
- Optimize one bottleneck while preserving lead quality.
- Record qualified inquiries and first-response time separately from clicks.

### Saturday — Authority asset

- Prepare one fact-checked off-site asset: LinkedIn post, buyer answer, short video outline, directory profile improvement, or digital-PR source note.
- Do not auto-publish, impersonate users, buy links, exchange links at scale, or use Reddit sockpuppets.

### Sunday — Weekly review

- Record KPI movement and data gaps.
- List wins, losses, indexing anomalies, new opportunity queries, and lead feedback.
- Select only three priorities for the next week.
- Refresh targets when four weeks of comparable data are available.

## Daily report format

1. Today's conclusion — one sentence.
2. Evidence — source, date range, URL/query, and observed value.
3. One primary action completed or proposed.
4. Validation — build/tests/live checks and deployment status.
5. KPI snapshot — index rate, non-brand impressions/clicks, top-20 buyer-intent queries, qualified leads, organic conversion rate, AI mentions; mark unavailable data as unavailable.
6. Tomorrow's priority.
7. Owner confirmation needed — no more than three questions.

## Content acceptance gate

A new page is publishable only when all applicable checks pass:

- It serves a distinct, real buyer decision and has a clear target audience.
- It adds LINHAO-specific or otherwise original value beyond search-result summaries.
- Product claims, dimensions, materials, MOQ, lead time, certification, factory, and customer statements are verified.
- It has a unique title/H1, canonical URL, descriptive metadata, useful image/alt text, and crawlable internal links.
- It provides a logical next step: product inquiry, WhatsApp, email, sample/catalog request, or related resource.
- It does not conflict with another page's primary intent.
- It is included in the sitemap only if it deserves indexing.

## Permanent red lines

- No guaranteed rankings or indexing claims.
- No paid link schemes, bulk directory spam, doorway pages, scraped rewrites, or scaled low-value AI pages.
- No fabricated certifications, tests, production data, customers, reviews, prices, MOQ, delivery times, or case studies.
- No automatic external publishing, outreach, advertising spend, or account changes without explicit authorization.
- No optimization based solely on traffic if inquiry relevance or trust declines.

## Review cadence

- Daily: one action plus evidence and KPI snapshot.
- Weekly: 30-minute Search Console, GA4, indexing, opportunity, conversion, and uptime review.
- Monthly: two-hour page/query/lead-quality review; update the next month's page and evidence backlog.
- Quarterly: full technical/content/competitor audit and one original data or buyer-decision asset.
